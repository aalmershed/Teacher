1. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Mualim Hub (مركز المعلم) is a productivity tool for school teachers in Saudi Arabia (Arabic interface). Teachers must keep a professional portfolio (ملف الإنجاز) with evidence of their work, and regularly prepare appreciation certificates for students and grade follow-up sheets. Doing this by hand in Word or on paper takes hours. The app gives teachers a ready-made portfolio template, certificate designs and grade sheets that they fill in from their phone, attach photos and files as evidence (each item gets an automatic QR code), preview, and export as print-ready PDF. The app is the mobile companion of the website https://mualimhub.com and uses the same account, so a teacher's documents sync between the website and the app.

2. HOW TO USE THE APP
- Demo account: the credentials are in the Sign-In Information fields. PDF export is enabled for this account.
- You can also create a new account from the login screen (سجّل مجانًا).
- Tab ملفاتي (My files): tap ＋ ملف إنجاز جديد to create a portfolio, open any page, edit text, tables and fields, and add evidence from Photos, Camera or Files (a QR code appears for each item). Use معاينة (Preview) and تنزيل PDF (Download PDF).
- Tab الشهادات (Certificates): create a certificate, choose a design, type one or more student names, see the live preview, download the PDF.
- Tab الكشوف (Sheets): create a follow-up sheet, add student names, tap the save button to switch to grade entry, tap the small boxes to mark ✓ / ✗ and type grades; totals and averages are calculated automatically.
- Tab حسابي (Account): profile, password, log out, and حذف الحساب (Delete account), which permanently deletes the account and all its files after password confirmation.
- No sample files are needed; any photo from the device can be used as evidence.

USER-GENERATED CONTENT
All content in the app is private to the teacher who created it. There is no feed, messaging, comments, profiles or any way to browse or see other users' content inside the app, so content reporting and blocking do not apply. Each teacher only sees their own documents after logging in.

PAID FEATURES
The app contains no purchases, prices, subscription offers or links to buy anything. Creating, editing and previewing are free for everyone. PDF export is available to accounts where it is enabled; for accounts without it, the app shows a neutral message that PDF download is not enabled for the account.

3. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Our own server at https://mualimhub.com (self-hosted Node.js server and database) for accounts, documents, uploaded files, QR codes and PDF generation.
- No third-party authentication, analytics, advertising, tracking, AI or payment services are used by the app.

4. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app works the same in all regions. The interface is in Arabic, aimed at teachers in Saudi Arabia.
